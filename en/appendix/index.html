Secondary evidence: useful context, separate counts

Classification follows the device actually used in the methods—not whether SYREBO appears in a title, introduction or reference list. Reviews, meta-analyses, commercial-device comparisons and adoption studies provide context but do not count as original clinical efficacy studies.

Kuvijitsuwan: air-guitar measurement study

Full text confirms a Chiang Mai University-built air-guitar. SYREBO appears only in an introductory commercial-device list. It is therefore secondary evidence, not direct engineering research.

Non-original-clinical issues in the earlier column

  • Ledoux third-party engineering comparison: comparative context, not an original SYREBO clinical efficacy study.
  • Japanese society review: secondary evidence and not interchangeable with original trials, case series or case reports.

Rules for reviews, meta-analyses and adoption studies

  • A category-level meta-analysis supports statements about soft robotic gloves as a class, not a SYREBO-specific trial claim.
  • Commercial comparisons require the exact tested models, comparators and outcomes; results cannot be extrapolated to untested models.
  • Adoption, usability and engineering studies retain their actual population and endpoints.
  • A trial protocol describes a planned study and is not a completed-study result.
