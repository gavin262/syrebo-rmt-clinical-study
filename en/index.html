Original clinical studies

Randomized pilot study · 2025

Robotic glove plus mirror therapy: the three-arm SY-HR06 pilot trial

66 randomized and 52 analyzed. The combined group had higher post-treatment FMA-UE than mirror therapy; no follow-up, and no glove-only attribution.

Yisheng SY-HR06 | Three arms | Four weeks
Clinical / neurophysiology study · 2023

Three-week passive robotic hand therapy: limited hand-function gains alongside neurophysiological changes

A clinical-training and neurophysiology exploration, not a confirmatory RCT demonstrating large hand-function gains. The abstract reports limited hand-function improvement and neurophysiological changes.

SY-HR08P | EEG + EMG | Three movement modes
Randomized controlled study · 2024

Fire-needle acupuncture plus robotic-glove mirror therapy: a four-week randomized study with 110 analyzed participants

Of 113 randomized participants, three dropped out and 110 were analyzed. Both groups received usual rehabilitation plus SY-HR03Y robotic-glove mirror therapy; the observation group additionally received fire-needle acupuncture. The between-group advantage belongs to the combined regimen.

Yisheng SY-HR03Y | 113 randomized | 110 analyzed
Randomized controlled study · 2026

Robotic-glove-assisted task-oriented mirror therapy: a 30-participant, eight-week non-blinded randomized trial

Thirty participants with subacute stroke were randomized to robotic-glove-assisted task-oriented mirror therapy or task-oriented mirror therapy alone. The abstract reports statistically significant within- and between-group differences; exact effect sizes require full-text verification.

SYREBO | 30 participants | BBT / FMA-UE / ARAT
Randomized controlled study · 2026

Robotic hand training with NMES: grip strength differed between groups; the pegboard between-group result did not

Forty-six participants with chronic stroke were randomized and 42 completed. Grip strength differed between groups after six weeks (p=0.002), but the affected-hand Purdue Pegboard between-group result was not significant (p=0.076).

SYREBO | 46 randomized | 42 completed

Mechanistic study

Mechanistic fNIRS study · 2024

Soft rehabilitation glove and virtual-reality scenes: brain activation in 20 healthy participants and 20 patients with stroke

This is a brain-activation mechanism study, not a clinical functional-efficacy trial. Twenty healthy participants and 20 patients with stroke completed robotic-glove-assisted passive grasping, VR grasping-video observation and the combined task.

fNIRS | RA / VR / VRA tasks | Not an efficacy trial

Engineering studies

Human–machine interface engineering · 2026

A-mode ultrasound for a soft hand exoskeleton: human–machine interfacing under active and passive conditions

This engineering feasibility study examines grasp-intent recognition under active and passive conditions. Institutional-repository excerpts report five healthy participants and seven grasps; it is not a stroke efficacy trial.

5 healthy participants | 7 grasps | HMI study
Pneumatic-source engineering · 2026

An electronics-free chemical-reaction pneumatic source: 7 bar, 6 L/min, 36°C and 40 dBA

The study presents an electronics-free pneumatic source integrating endothermic–exothermic chemical reactions with passive mechanical regulation. The abstract reports 7 bar, 6 L/min, a maximum temperature of 36°C and 40 dBA. It addresses soft-robot power supply, not patient outcomes.

Chemical pneumatic source | Near-silent | Low heat
